# Wild Yough Glamping — Direct Booking Site & Guest Portal

Direct-booking website for **Wild Yough Glamping**, a 5-hut glampground on 350 private acres along the Youghiogheny River in Oakland, Maryland.

🔗 Live: [dclconcierge.com/wyg](https://dclconcierge.com/wyg)

## Goal
Shift bookings from third-party platforms (which take fees and hide guest contact details) to direct reservations on the business's own site.

## Features
### Live
- **Hut comparison table** — amenities, capacity, privacy level, bathhouse distance and accessibility across all 5 huts
- **Dynamic quote calculator** — night-by-night pricing that applies weekend (Fri–Sat) vs. mid-week (Sun–Thu) rates, single-hut vs. full-site pricing, 6% Maryland + 8% Garrett County tax, and the initial deposit due
- **Filterable guest reviews** by hut
- **Lead capture** — mailing-list discount signup and partnership inquiry form

### In development
- **Online reservations** — guests book and pay directly on the site
- **Guest dashboard** — guests look up and confirm their reservation, request changes, and add on services (e.g. extra cots, pets, concierge extras)

## Skills demonstrated
- Pricing logic and rule-based calculations in JavaScript
- Structuring operational data (units, rates, amenities) for display and comparison
- UX for a conversion-focused booking flow
- HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WordPress (Kubio)
