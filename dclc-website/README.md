# Deep Creek Lake Concierge — Company Website

Website for **Deep Creek Lake Concierge (DCLC)**, a vacation-services and property-support company at Deep Creek Lake, Maryland.

🔗 Live: [dclconcierge.com](https://dclconcierge.com)

![Homepage screenshot](screenshots/home.png)

## My role
Website maintenance, redesign and data — building custom sections, forms and integrations on top of WordPress.

## Features
- **Interactive service catalog** — clickable tiles for 8 service lines
- **Tiered pricing** — Essentials, Signature Concierge and Premier packages
- **Quote request form** with validation, success/error states and service-level selection — captures structured leads
- **Photo gallery** with lightbox, testimonials, team section
- Links into the [Wild Yough Glamping booking site](../wyg-booking-site)

## Skills demonstrated
- Custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript components inside WordPress
- Form design for structured lead data
- Information architecture for a multi-service business
